# Folio Manifesto

**Document ID:** FOLIO-PRODUCT-001  
**Status:** Agreed product identity  
**Product:** Folio  
**Project format:** .flop

## The manifesto

> Menghapus batas antara keterbatasan alat dan kebesaran karya. Bukan untuk menggantikan developer, melainkan membangun taman bermain baru bagi mereka.

This is the founding statement of Folio. It expresses both the reason Folio exists and the spirit that must remain intact as its technology, interface, and team evolve.

## What this means

### 1. A person's tools must not define the size of their ambition

Folio exists as hope for creators and developers who face limited facilities, hardware, or resources. High-quality digital work should not be reserved only for people with expensive equipment or access to large production teams.

Folio should respond through efficient architecture, lightweight workflows, progressive capabilities, and careful use of resources. This is a design commitment, not an unsupported promise that every feature will run equally well on every device.

### 2. Folio is a playground for developers, not a replacement for them

Folio must expand what designers and developers can create together. It should give visual creators an expressive workspace while preserving developers' freedom to work directly with code, logic, architecture, and implementation details.

The goal is not to remove developers from the process. The goal is to give them a richer environment in which ideas can be explored visually, refined technically, and connected to a real project.

### 3. Creative freedom and engineering integrity belong together

Folio should feel exploratory and expressive without becoming careless about data, code, compatibility, or reliability. A playful workspace must be built on dependable native foundations.

### 4. One project, many ways to create

Design, code, behavior, backend work, preview, and future collaboration should be connected parts of one project. Different workspaces may have different workflows, but they must respect shared project contracts.

## The promise we must preserve

- Do not position Folio as a tool intended to eliminate developers.
- Do not make access to sophisticated creation depend unnecessarily on high-end hardware.
- Do not sacrifice project integrity for visual convenience.
- Do not confuse a polished interface with a mature underlying engine.
- Do not let future feature growth erase the original purpose of Folio.
